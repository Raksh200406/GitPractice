My Favourite Movies

Interstellar

Interstellar is a science-fiction movie about astronauts who travel through space to find a new planet for humans. The movie explores space, time, love and human survival.

Released Year: 2014

Hero: Matthew McConaughey

Heroine: Anne Hathaway

Type: Science Fiction, Drama

3 Idiots

3 Idiots is an Indian comedy-drama movie about three engineering students and their college life. The movie talks about friendship, education, career pressure and following your passion.

Released Year: 2009

Hero: Aamir Khan

Heroine: Kareena Kapoor

Type: Comedy, Drama

Avengers: Endgame

Avengers: Endgame follows the Avengers as they try to undo the destruction caused by Thanos. The heroes come together for one final mission to save the universe.

Released Year: 2019

Hero: Robert Downey Jr.

Heroine: Scarlett Johansson

Type: Action, Adventure, Science Fiction

KGF

KGF is an action movie about Rocky, a man who rises from poverty and becomes a powerful figure in the gold mining fields of Kolar. The movie focuses on ambition, power and revenge.

Released Year: 2018

Hero: Yash

Heroine: Srinidhi Shetty

Type: Action, Drama

Spider-Man: No Way Home

Spider-Man: No Way Home follows Peter Parker after his identity is revealed to the world. He asks Doctor Strange for help, but the spell causes villains and characters from different universes to appear.

Released Year: 2021

Hero: Tom Holland

Heroine: Zendaya

Type: Action, Adventure, Science Fiction
